# IBM – Quantum Computing Credentials

This section presents four completed credentials issued by IBM and IBM SkillsBuild in October 2026.

## 1. Using a Quantum Computer

- **Provider:** IBM
- **Issue Date:** 24 October 2026

**Description:** Covers the key components of Qiskit, quantum circuits, Estimator and Sampler primitives, and mapping real-world problems to qubits, with an emphasis on Hamiltonian simulation.

## 2. Foundations of Quantum Error Correction

- **Provider:** IBM
- **Issue Date:** 24 October 2026

**Description:** Covers quantum errors and error-correction methods, including the nine-qubit Shor code, advanced quantum error-correcting codes, error-rate thresholds, and code distance.

## 3. General Formulation of Quantum Information

- **Provider:** IBM
- **Issue Date:** 24 October 2026

**Description:** Covers density matrices, quantum channels, and mathematical representations used to describe, preserve, and manipulate quantum information.

## 4. Quantum Enigmas

- **Provider:** IBM SkillsBuild
- **Issue Date:** 26 October 2026

**Description:** Introduces quantum computing fundamentals, including superposition, entanglement, measurement, quantum logic gates, and practical exercises using IBM Quantum Composer.
