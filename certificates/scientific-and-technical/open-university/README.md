# The Open University – Completed Courses

This section documents six completed courses offered by The Open University, covering scientific and technical subjects, data analysis, quantum computing, computational thinking, and leadership.

- **Provider:** The Open University
- **Duration:** 20 hours
- **Issue Date:** 29 September 2026
- **Description:** Introduction to graphical representations of data and numerical methods for summarising datasets.

## 2. An Introduction to Electronics

- **Provider:** The Open University

- **Provider:** The Open University

## 5. Collective Leadership

- **Issue Date:** 5 October 2026

## 6. Inclusive Leadership: Effecting Change

- **Provider:** The Open University
- **Duration:** 8 hours
