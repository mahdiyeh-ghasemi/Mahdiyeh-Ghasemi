# NIST – Cybersecurity and Risk Management Courses

This section documents three completed introductory courses offered by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), covering risk management, security and privacy controls, and security control baselines.

## 1. Risk Management Framework for Systems and Organizations — Introductory Course, Version 2.0

- **Provider:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- **Issue Date:** September 2026

**Description:** Introduction to the Risk Management Framework (RMF) for systems and organizations, including the management of security and privacy risks.

## 2. Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations — Introductory Course

- **Provider:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- **Issue Date:** April 2024

**Description:** Introduction to security and privacy controls for information systems and organizations.

## 3. Control Baselines for Information Systems and Organizations — Introductory Course

- **Provider:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- **Issue Date:** April 2024

**Description:** Introduction to security control baselines for information systems and organizations.
