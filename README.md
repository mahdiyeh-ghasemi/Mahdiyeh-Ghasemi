
# Mahdiyeh Ghasemi

### Physicist | Experimental Photonics | Quantum Systems | Computational Physics

I am a physicist with an academic background in solid-state physics and atomic and molecular physics, and research experience in experimental photonics, laser spectroscopy, and quantum systems.

My research experience spans experimental investigations of laser–matter interaction and optical emission spectroscopy, as well as theoretical and computational studies of quantum systems. I have worked with Laser-Induced Breakdown Spectroscopy (LIBS) for material analysis and investigated quantum dot systems using operator methods and exact diagonalization.

I enjoy exploring the connection between fundamental physics, experimental research, and computational modeling. I am particularly interested in understanding physical phenomena through both theoretical approaches and practical implementation, and in developing ideas into meaningful scientific applications.
